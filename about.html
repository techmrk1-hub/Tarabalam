ABOUT THE PLATFORM

About Bramha.org

A digital home for verified Śāstra, Vedic resources and traditional knowledge — preserving provenance while making serious study easier to access.

Mission

Bramha.org organizes traditional source material into a clear digital structure for reading, reference and gradual scholarly expansion.

Verification Policy

A database record is never public merely because it exists. Public Śāstra pages expose only content explicitly marked Verified and approved for publication.

Editorial Policy

Source text, translation, commentary, cross references and editorial notes remain distinct layers. Source-supported corrections stay traceable; unsupported readings are not silently invented.

SOURCE-GROUNDED DIGITAL STUDY

Tradition first. Technology in service of clarity.

The platform uses a structured publishing workflow so the public reader remains fast and convenient without separating the text from its source and verification history.
