About the Platform

About Bramha.org

A digital home for verified Śāstra, Vedic resources and traditional knowledge — designed to preserve provenance while making serious study easier.

Mission

Bramha.org organizes traditional source material into a clear digital structure for reading, reference and gradual scholarly expansion.

Verification Policy

A database row is not automatically public. Only records marked Verified and explicitly approved for publication are exposed by the public reader.

Editorial Policy

Source text, translation, commentary, cross references and editorial notes are kept as distinct layers. Source-supported corrections should remain traceable and should never be silently invented.

Technology

The public site uses a static web front end with a structured data service behind it. This keeps the reading experience fast while allowing controlled publishing and future user features.
