About Bramha.org

A digital Śāstra knowledge library for reading, comparing and citing traditional knowledge. Prepared by Mahankali Ravikiran Sharma.

What this is

Texts, topics, commentaries, cross references and research. The public site is a library, not a school, a course list, or an enrolment desk.

Verification

A row is public only when its verification status is Verified and its publish flag is YES. Drafts stay in the database.

Editorial policy

Source text, translation, commentary, Prayoga and notes remain separate. Missing commentary is left missing. Sanskrit is not silently rewritten.

How a record is published

Editors maintain the Google Sheet. The site reads that sheet, keeps a Supabase snapshot for structured data, and regenerates permanent pages for verified rows.
