About Bramha.org

A digital home for verified Śāstra, Vedic resources, and traditional knowledge — preserving provenance while making serious study easier to access. Prepared by Mahankali Ravikiran Sharma as a student-friendly library and traditional timing workshop.

Mission

Bramha.org organizes traditional source material into a clear digital structure for reading, reference, and gradual scholarly expansion.

Verification

A database record is never public merely because it exists. Public pages expose only content marked Verified and approved for publication.

Editorial policy

Source text, translation, commentary, cross-references, and notes remain distinct layers. Unsupported readings are not silently invented.
