Knowledge layers

One passage can hold several layers. A layer appears only when that field exists in the verified record.

  1. Original textDevanagari, Telugu script, and IAST or transliteration.
  2. PadacchedaWord division, when the source record includes it.
  3. Word meaningWord-by-word meaning in the language supplied by the record.
  4. TranslationEnglish translation and Telugu meaning, kept distinct.
  5. Simple explanationA plain restatement, separate from commentary.
  6. CommentaryBhāṣya, vṛtti, ṭīkā, or another commentary only when a verified record exists.
  7. PrayogaContext of use, without turning the library into a manual of invention.
  8. Cross referencesRelated passages and notes, including variants when recorded.
  9. SourceEdition, page, and verification status.
